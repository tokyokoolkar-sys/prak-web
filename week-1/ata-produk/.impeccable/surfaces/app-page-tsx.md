# Surface: Home Hero

## Scope
Persuade surface for quant professionals. The first viewport presents Lotus Quant as an AI risk copilot and invites exploration of the portfolio workspace.

## Audience and Job
Quant professionals need to turn research into controlled, observable execution. They should understand the product, inspect an illustrative risk state, and choose the primary workspace action within seconds. Commercial proof and performance claims remain unavailable.

## Direction
Optical Risk Field: a light, minimal analytical surface where translucent signal channels bend around a cursor-driven control point. The pointer acts as an analyst placing a stress lens over portfolio context; the AI rationale changes with the selected risk state. Synthetic data remains labeled.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A cursor should act as an analytical instrument over portfolio risk, not a decoration; Lotus Quant lets a quant move attention through the model and see the resulting reasoning.
OWN-WORLD: paper-white field, graphite typography, hairline construction marks, translucent cobalt and vermilion signal channels, small mono measurements, soft optical depth without glass cards.
STORY: A quant enters a calm workspace, moves the pointer through a portfolio risk field, changes the stress state, reads the copilot rationale, and opens the cockpit.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A sparse 72px header, large left-side offer and actions, a wide cursor-reactive analytical field on the right and below, with one concise copilot conclusion attached to the field.
FORM: User-pinned light shader refinement, retaining the product mechanism; seed 90f82e00.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
