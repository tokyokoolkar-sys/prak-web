# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Quant professionals who need to turn research into controlled, observable execution through risk and portfolio decisions.

## Product Purpose

Lotus Quant gives quant professionals an AI risk copilot for portfolio work. Success means traders can understand risk, interrogate portfolio context, and make better-informed decisions with the AI beside them rather than in place of them.

## Positioning

Lotus Quant brings human market judgment and LLM analysis into one live decision surface for risk and portfolio management.

## Operating Context

The product is experienced in a professional market-analysis workflow where portfolio context, risk signals, and AI reasoning need to remain legible at the same time.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The initial surface must demonstrate an AI risk copilot, portfolio context, risk signals, and a route into a portfolio cockpit. Commercial claims, performance benchmarks, customer evidence, pricing, and production capability claims remain undecided and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Lotus Quant. The initial positioning is precise, analytical, and expert-led rather than mystical or consumer-friendly.

## Evidence on Hand

No Lotus Quant logo, product screenshots, testimonials, benchmarks, customer evidence, or brand assets are available in the repository. Hero metrics and chart values must be labeled synthetic where a visitor could mistake them for live or measured data.

## Product Principles

- Put decision context before decoration.
- Preserve human judgment while making machine reasoning inspectable.
- Show the portfolio consequence of a signal, not the signal alone.
- Make risk understandable at professional speed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience should support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion preferences, and legible chart labels.
